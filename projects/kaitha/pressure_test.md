## Attention Map (`AUDIENCE_ADVOCATE`) — dip-risk zones
- **0:00–0:28** — plays as quiet romance; misdirection is intended but needs a *question* humming. Fix: 1–2 early "tells" (a held look at the pot; her warmth visibly switching off) so curiosity carries the first act.
- **1:15–1:25 (SOLO)** — highest sag risk, carried by interiority. Needs +1–2 micro-reversals beyond the boat-light fakeout (a sound from the rocks, a dropped roll she must retrieve, headlights on the far road).

## Authenticity (`SME_POOL`)
- **"Nothing comes back from out there"** — a wrapped, bludgeoned body can resurface; tides return things. Earn the line (weights / known deep current) or carry it as ticking dread.
- **Krish's exposure** — a uniformed guard whose patrol coincides with a disappearance + torched hut + vanished wife is the obvious prime suspect. Intended (he's "the price"), but the film should know it.
- **The phone** — "no current reaches this far" (dead TV, kerosene) vs. a charged mobile. Plug it (charged in town / small solar panel).
- Verify tide / "third bar" / current and sea-guard boat-and-uniform procedure with a real southern-Kerala source.

## Dissent (`DEVILS_ADVOCATE`) — highest-value note
**The money is partly Krish's — and the draft doesn't use it.** He's given her weekly rolls "for *our* field"; they're in that pot. When it shatters / he sees the stacked cash, does he recognize his own savings? Right now he barely registers it. If Krish realizes the fund he thought was theirs is what she killed for — and is taking — and helps her anyway, the betrayal goes from sad to devastating. Do not leave this on the floor.
