## §1 Verdict

The sweep **confirms v5's originality**: the closest engines in ~1,100 works — `natsume` (an inherited object binding many spirits), `the-overcoat` (dead man + stolen cloth), `sizwe-banzi-is-dead` (living inside a dead man's identity-object), Othello's handkerchief (the stolen charged cloth) — all differ from THORTH on the load-bearing axis: **consent and shelter**. Nothing in the database replaces anything in THORTH; nothing needs to. What the database contributes is (a) a dozen fold-now mechanics that sharpen beats v5 already has, (b) a complete, largely public-domain anthology slate for the coda's promise, (c) a ready-made differentiation bible for every comparison reviewers and platforms will make, and (d) format/business precedents for the franchise shape.

## §2 FOLD-NOW — twelve grafts worth writing into v5 / the bible now

*(All mechanics, no premises; PD marked where the surface itself is free.)*

1. **`before-the-law` (Kafka, PD) → Rule 10a.** Why the dead queue and never storm: each unhoused soul perceives the cloth-light as a gate meant only for it, and waits, procedure-bound. One bible line closes the "why don't they rush the hostel" question in-cosmology — the queue-not-army discipline gets its doctrine.
2. **`kwaidan` — the Hoichi mechanic (PD) → Ep7.** Protection is only as complete as its coverage: the students' circle has one unmarked gap (a lapsed attention, an unheld hand), and the breach is *priced* — knots spent to cover it. The rite becomes survivable-by-discipline instead of tableau.
3. **`the-oresteia` — the Eumenides settlement (PD) → Ep7 housing dramaturgy.** The pursuing spirit is not banished but formally offered place and honor, and consent converts wrath to blessing. The girl's housing: Papa's crossing is the *offer made legible*; her taking his hand is consent spoken in gesture. The classical proof that "housed, not defeated" plays as climax.
4. **`samskara` (R — mechanics) → Ep7 doctrinal wavering.** The rite-eligibility crisis: by what law does a Kerala plathi knot a *Rajasthani* soul — "until the land accepts you again," but this was never her land? The wavering gains a doctrinal edge, and Papa's answer widens his own rite: any tended ground is the land. One line, and the climax's emotion gets a theology.
5. **`doctor-faustus` + `the-rocking-horse-winner` (both PD) → Ep2–3 under-rhythm.** Faustus: the audible countdown beneath conjured wins (the clock-strike grammar). Lawrence: the wins are *wired to the hunger* — every luck-tilt brightens the beacon and swells the whisper. Together they causally fuse the fun of the luck era to the siege it summons.
6. **`the-monkeys-paw` + `the-happy-prince` (both PD) → the ledger's endpoint.** The hem holds only so many knots (a finite, countable cap), and depletion runs to a terminal remainder — the first knot as the leaden heart, the last thing left. The prop bible gains an arc and an ending, not just states.
7. **`the-sorcerers-apprentice` + `the-trickster-of-seville` (both PD) → Ep4 parade teeth.** Each charlatan's half-known rite *escalates measurably* what it mocks (new knots, more woken dead — comedy with a ratchet); and the TV exorcist's theatrical challenge — "come then, spirit!" — is the one summons that works, feeding the strikes-only-predators rule.
8. **`translations` + `chinatown` (R — mechanics) → the investigation's forensic spine.** The erased savaparambu survives inside a corrupted plot-name in the land records (the oral name is a dead man's story — Kannan's beat gains a document), and the ground was "sold" under the signatures of men already buried in it — the dead made to sign their own eviction. Theme fused to procedure; pure 2006 registry texture.
9. **`oh-whistle-and-ill-come` (PD) → Ep6 manifestation grammar.** The clothless dead borrow cloth to be seen: the hostel's own drying lines and bedsheets turning their faces. Cloth-native escalation that complements (does not duplicate) the room-by-room cession — in a show about a towel, every towel in the building becomes suspect.
10. **`mother-courage` (PD) → prop-bible law.** The wagon-ledger discipline: the camera counts the knots; the characters never remark on them (until Hari's Ep4 counting scene breaks the silence deliberately). One sentence that protects the ledger from over-writing.
11. **`riders-to-the-sea` (PD) → Rule 1 deepened + finale image.** Cloth read as the body's ledger: knots are plathi signatures — each thorthu readable, each dead namable. The kavu becomes an archive and the queue a census; the hundreds-of-cloths finale quietly becomes a library of lives.
12. **`the-caucasian-chalk-circle` (PD) → the handover's adjudication.** The chalk-circle release test: the true keeper is the one who refuses to pull. A wordless grammar for Ep7's handover — Hari's letting-go is itself the proof of his claim, which is exactly Rule 2 staged.

*Supporting the audit's #1 finding:* **`frieren` (R)** — the posthumous-portrait mechanic (assembling a dead man from places, objects, and others' memories until the audience grieves someone never seen alive) is the craft model for the living-Papa dose; **`ikiru`** does the same via wake-testimony and also serves the girl's story if the flashback ever needs replacing with witnesses.

## §5 The differentiation bible (what THORTH is *not* — write these down before reviewers do)

**The five that need formal one-pagers:**
- **`natsume` (Book of Friends)** — the closest engine in the entire database: an inherited object binding many spirits, drawing them to its holder, a guardian attached to the object, one release per episode. *Differentiation: the Book subjugates (names taken in defeat); the thorthu shelters by consent — and THORTH compresses the release-per-episode ledger into one siege and one rite.*
- **`tumbbad`** — the inevitable Indian folk-horror comp. *Its cosmology monetises greed (feed the god, take); THORTH's monetises care (house the dead, give back).*
- **`kantara`** — the folk-rite + land-dispute comp every platform will name. *Kantara's deity fights in fury; THORTH's cloth shelters in grief.*
- **`coco`** — the nearest mainstream dead-cosmology. *Remembrance vs. residence: Coco's dead need to be remembered; THORTH's need to be housed.*
- **`pet-sematary` / `the-haunting-of-hill-house`** — the burial-ground and hungry-house genre expectations to defuse early. *Nothing comes back wrong here; the ground shelters the lonely instead of consuming them.*

## §6 Comps & business models

- **`the-white-lotus`** — the place-anthology season model: new ground, constant format, one crisis per season. The business shape of the coda's promise.

- **`arabian-nights` / `the-decameron`** — the PD frame precedents; `duanju-campus-genius` — the vertical cutdown grammar for the luck era, if ever needed.
- **⚠️ `the-blair-witch-project` — adopt with caution.** The myth-as-marketing playbook (seeding folklore as ambiguously real) directly conflicts with THORTH's own compliance rule (§8: marketing never claims "based on a real ritual" — sacred groves are real, living institutions).
