SECTION 1 — DATA CONTRACT

4H: OPEN, HIGH, LOW, CLOSE, VOLUME (at least 100 bars)
1H: OPEN, HIGH, LOW, CLOSE, VOLUME (real-time)

SECTION 2 — MULTI-TIMEFRAME ANALYSIS
Daily trend: EMA_50_daily vs EMA_200_daily alignment
4H structure: swing highs/lows for entry timing
1H execution: precise entry on pullback or breakout
Higher timeframe filter: daily EMA_50 > EMA_200 → only longs allowed

SECTION 3 — SWING STRUCTURE
SWING_HIGH = HIGH[i] > HIGH[i-1] AND HIGH[i] > HIGH[i+1] (confirmed pivot)
SWING_LOW = LOW[i] < LOW[i-1] AND LOW[i] < LOW[i+1] (confirmed pivot)
Higher High: current SWING_HIGH > previous SWING_HIGH → uptrend
Higher Low: current SWING_LOW > previous SWING_LOW → uptrend continuation
Lower High / Lower Low → downtrend
PIVOT = (HIGH + LOW + CLOSE) / 3 (daily pivot point)
ATR_DAILY = ATR(14) on daily bars

SECTION 4 — ENTRY RULES
Swing long conditions (ALL required):
  1. Daily: EMA_50 > EMA_200 (bullish trend)
  2. 4H: price at higher low level OR breakout above swing high
  3. 1H: bullish engulfing or pin bar at support
  4. Volume: above 20-day average on signal bar
  5. No earnings/news within 2 trading days

SECTION 5 — POSITION SIZING
R_MULTIPLE = |entry - stop| / ATR_DAILY (risk per unit of volatility)
Position size = risk_per_trade / |entry - stop|
Risk per trade: 1.0% of account (max)
Max portfolio heat (total open risk): 5% of account

SECTION 6 — PARTIAL EXITS
Scale-out plan (deterministic):
  T1 (first target): 1R → close 33% of position (PARTIAL_SIZE = 0.33)
  T2 (second target): 2R → close 33% of position
  T3 (final target): trail remainder with ATR-based stop
PARTIAL_SIZE = position_fraction_to_close at each target

SECTION 7 — GAP RISK
GAP_RISK = max_historical_gap_pct * position_value
Overnight position rules:
  - Max 50% of normal position size held overnight
  - IF earnings within 2 days → no overnight hold
  - IF GAP_RISK > 2% of account → reduce to 25% position
  - Pre-market gap > 2*ATR_DAILY → reassess stop on open
Weekend hold: only if weekly trend strongly aligned AND position profitable

SECTION 8 — FSM
States: FLAT, SETUP, ENTRY, HOLDING, PARTIAL, EXIT

Transitions:

ENTRY → HOLDING: position confirmed, stops placed
HOLDING → PARTIAL: T1 target reached, first scale-out executed
PARTIAL → EXIT: final target OR trailing stop hit OR max holding period

Max holding period: 10 trading days (if no target hit → market exit)
