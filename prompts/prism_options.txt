SECTION 2 — GREEKS ENGINE
DELTA = dV/dS (option price sensitivity to underlying)
GAMMA = dDelta/dS (rate of delta change)
THETA = dV/dt (time decay per day)
VEGA = dV/dIV (sensitivity to implied volatility)
Rho = dV/dr (sensitivity to interest rate)
Portfolio Greeks: sum of individual position Greeks

SECTION 3 — VOLATILITY SURFACE
IV_RANK = (current_IV - 52wk_low_IV) / (52wk_high_IV - 52wk_low_IV) * 100
IV skew analysis: compare put IV vs call IV at same delta
Vol smile: IV across strikes at same expiration
High IV environment: IV_RANK > 50 → favor selling strategies
Low IV environment: IV_RANK < 30 → favor buying strategies

SECTION 4 — STRATEGY DEFINITIONS
Defined-risk strategies:
  Vertical spread: buy + sell same type, different strikes
  Iron condor: sell OTM put spread + sell OTM call spread
  Butterfly: buy 1 lower + sell 2 middle + buy 1 upper
  Straddle: buy call + put at same strike (long vol)
  Strangle: buy OTM call + OTM put (wide long vol)
  Calendar spread: sell front month + buy back month (same strike)

SECTION 5 — ENTRY RULES
Iron Condor entry (ALL must be true):
  1. IV_RANK > 50 (high volatility environment)
  2. DTE (days to expiration) = 30-45 days
  3. Short strikes at 16-delta (1 standard deviation)
  4. Width = 1-2 strikes beyond short strikes
  5. Credit received >= 1/3 of wing width
Assignment risk: monitor when DTE < 7 AND ITM — close or roll

SECTION 6 — POSITION SIZING
MAX_LOSS = wing_width - credit_received (per contract)
Position size: MAX_LOSS <= 3% of portfolio per trade
Max portfolio allocation to options: 30%
BREAKEVEN = short_strike ± credit_received (for credit spreads)

SECTION 7 — ADJUSTMENT RULES
Adjustment triggers (deterministic):
  IF DELTA of position exceeds ±0.30 → adjust
  IF underlying breaches short strike → roll untested side
  IF DTE < 21 AND position profitable > 50% → close early
  IF loss exceeds 2x credit received → close position

SECTION 8 — EXIT RULES
Profit target: close at 50% of max profit
Max loss: close at 2x credit received
Expiration management: close all positions by DTE = 3 (never hold to expiration)
Time-based: IF DTE < 7 → close regardless of P&L

SECTION 9 — FSM
States: SCANNING, PRICED, LEGGED_IN, MANAGING, ADJUSTING, CLOSED

Transitions:
SCANNING → PRICED: IV_RANK and underlying conditions met
PRICED → LEGGED_IN: all legs filled within acceptable slippage
LEGGED_IN → MANAGING: position confirmed, Greeks calculated
MANAGING → ADJUSTING: adjustment trigger hit
MANAGING → CLOSED: profit target or max loss reached
ADJUSTING → MANAGING: adjustment completed, new Greeks recalculated
CLOSED → SCANNING: cooldown complete, ready for new opportunity
