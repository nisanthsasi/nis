# Open Questions for the Principal

The committee cannot responsibly shortlist 10 ventures until the
Principal answers the following. Answers will be recorded here and
in `committee/DECISIONS.md`.

## 1. Capital & runway
- How much **starting capital** is available (USD equivalent)?
- What is the **maximum monthly burn** you will tolerate before
  revenue?
- Are you willing to take on debt or outside investment, or is this
  bootstrap-only?

## 2. Time & involvement
- How many hours per week will you (the human) actually spend?
- Which tasks are you willing to do personally (sign contracts,
  answer a weekly review, record a video, talk to a customer),
  and which must be 100% automated?

## 3. Jurisdiction & legal
- What country / state will the businesses be registered in?
- Do you already have an LLC / company, or do we start from zero?
- Any industries that are **off-limits** for you (e.g. crypto,
  adult, gambling, health claims, financial advice, firearms)?

## 4. Skills & assets you bring
- What existing skills, audiences, domains, code, or customer
  relationships can we leverage on day one?
- Any languages other than English you can operate in?

## 5. Risk appetite
- Prefer **10 small steady** cashflow businesses (boring, $500–$5k/mo
  each), or **a mix** including 1–2 swing-for-the-fences bets?
- Comfortable with businesses that depend on third-party platforms
  (Amazon, App Store, Etsy, Google, Meta), or do you want
  platform-independent only?

## 6. Ethics & brand
- Any hard ethical lines beyond what's legal? (e.g. "no ads to
  children", "no AI-generated art sold as human", "no dropshipping
  from unknown suppliers".)
- Should the 10 ventures share a public brand/network, or stay
  independent and unlinked?

## 7. Tech preferences
- Preferred cloud (AWS / GCP / Azure / Cloudflare / Vercel)?
- Preferred LLM provider(s) and any data-residency constraints?
- Okay to use third-party SaaS (Stripe, Shopify, Zapier, etc.)
  or must everything be self-hosted?

## 8. Success definition
- What does "success" look like at **day 90**? (e.g. "3 of 10
  ventures cashflow-positive, total $2k MRR")
- What does it look like at **day 365**?
- What's the number at which you'd quit your day job (if applicable)?
