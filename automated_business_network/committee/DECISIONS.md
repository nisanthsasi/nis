   - Entity: Indian LLP (cheap, simple) or Pvt Ltd (needed for foreign investment).

   - **Cost advantage**: LLM API costs, hosting, and labor are lower.
   - **WhatsApp-first**: 500M+ Indian users; WhatsApp Business API is a real channel.
   - Time zone: GMT+5:30 — well-positioned for US async work and EU overlap.

2. **Bootstrap budget** eliminates capital-heavy plays:
   - No algo trading (capital risk), no physical products, no paid-ad-first models.
   - Focus on digital, service, and content businesses with near-zero COGS.

3. **15+ hrs/week** is generous:
   - Can do customer discovery, sales calls, content creation, and weekly ops.
   - This unlocks agency/service models that <2 hr/week wouldn't allow.
   - Can personally handle onboarding for the first 10 customers of each venture.

4. **No off-limits** gives maximum optionality:
   - Committee will still flag legal/ethical risks per the charter.
   - Crypto, health, finance verticals are on the table but with risk notes.
