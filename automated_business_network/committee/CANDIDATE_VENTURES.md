# Candidate Ventures (Long List)

This is the committee's initial brainstorm. It will be cut to 10
after the Principal answers `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. Each candidate is
rated 1–5 on five axes:

- **M** Margin potential
- **A** Automatability (how close to hands-off)
- **C** Capital required (5 = very low)
- **L** Legal/compliance simplicity (5 = low risk)
- **T** Time-to-first-dollar (5 = fast)

Total = sum (max 25). Higher = better fit for an automated network.
